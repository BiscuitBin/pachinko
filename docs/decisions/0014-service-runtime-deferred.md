# Decision 0014: The service runtime waits for the specifications

This record will explain why the service is a minimal std binary with no async runtime,
transport or HTTP stack yet: choosing them before the specifications say what the
service must do would be a guess. A spike and a further decision follow the
specifications. It will cover the context, the decision, its consequences and what would
reopen it.
